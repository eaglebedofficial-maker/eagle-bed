Frequently asked questions

What size is a UK small double bed?

A UK small double is 4ft wide and takes a 120 x 190 cm mattress. A standard double is 4ft6, or 135 x 190 cm.

Is a small double big enough for two people?

Two adults would have about 60 cm each, which many couples find tight. A small double is best for one adult, a teenager or a guest room; for two adults consider a double or king size bed.

Is delivery free on small double beds?

Yes. Delivery is free across Great Britain, and Northern Ireland is £90. You can return an unused, unassembled bed in its original packaging within 14 days, and we collect it free.
