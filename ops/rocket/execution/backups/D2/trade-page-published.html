Sizes and options

  • Sizes: Single (3ft), Small Double (4ft), Double (4ft 6in), King (5ft) and Super King (6ft). Kids beds come in the sizes shown on each product page.
  • Approximate weights: Single about 30 kg, Small Double about 36 kg, Double about 40 kg, King about 45 kg, Super King about 50 kg.
  • Every frame has a 5-year warranty. Our mattresses have a 5-year guarantee.
  • Optional extras (room-of-choice drop off £29.99, room-of-choice assembly £69.99) are chosen on each product page.
