# Sales drop diagnosis (23 Nov 2026)

## Orders by week (Shopify)
- w/c 26 Oct: 3 orders, £828
- w/c 2 Nov: 6 orders, £2,811
- w/c 9 Nov: 5 orders, £2,316
- w/c 16 Nov: 1 order, £540

## Google Ads (PMax is the only active campaign, £25/day, Maximise Conversions)
- Weekly spend was flat: about £175 (w/c 2 Nov), £182 (w/c 9 Nov) and £190 (w/c 16 Nov).
- Google-attributed orders per week were flat too: 1, 1, 1, 0.
- Last 28 days: £693 spent, 1,086 clicks, 4.96 conversions worth £1,972, ROAS 2.85 on revenue.
- PROFITABILITY NOT VERIFIED: the cost data is parked.

## What dropped
- Orders with no referrer ("direct", meaning returning visitors, phone or WhatsApp): 5, then 4, then 1.
- Google search sessions held steady (161 / 191 / 151 sessions). Their add-to-carts fell: 22 / 31 / 15.
- The direct-session spike in w/c 16 Nov (628 sessions) was mostly bots.

## Likely causes (hypotheses; the numbers are too small to prove any of them)
1. Shoppers waiting for Black Friday (27 Nov) while the store had no offer.
2. Normal week-to-week swings when volume is this low (1 to 6 orders a week).

## Action taken (owner approved)
- 10% off everything, automatic, from 23 Nov to 30 Nov.
- Google Ads unchanged at £25/day. Review on 1 Dec.
