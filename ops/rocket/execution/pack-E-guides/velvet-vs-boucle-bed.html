Velvet vs Bouclé Bed: Look, Feel and Care Compared

The short answer: choose velvet if you want a smooth, rich finish with a soft sheen that looks smart in deep colours. Choose bouclé if you want a cosy, textured, matt look that feels relaxed and hides small everyday marks. Both are soft to the touch. The main practical differences are how they reflect light, how they show marks and how they get on with pets. The easiest way to decide is to hold both fabrics in your own bedroom, which is why we send free fabric swatches.

What is velvet?

Velvet is a woven fabric with a short, dense pile. The cut fibres stand up together and give the surface its smooth feel and soft sheen. When you brush your hand across velvet, the colour shifts slightly lighter or darker depending on the direction of the pile. This is part of its appeal, because it gives an upholstered headboard depth and a slightly glamorous look.

It works particularly well on panel, Chesterfield and wingback headboards, where the stitching and shaping catch the light. What is bouclé?

Bouclé takes its name from the French word for "curled". It is made from looped yarn, so the surface has small, irregular curls instead of a flat pile. The result is a matt, textured fabric that looks soft and inviting. Teddy bouclé has a fuller, fluffier loop that is often compared to a teddy bear's coat.

How each fabric looks in a real bedroom

Light makes a bigger difference than most people expect. Velvet reflects light, so the same colour can look brighter by a window and darker in a shaded corner. In a room with lots of daylight, a mid-tone velvet can look lively and expensive. In a darker room, a deep green, navy or charcoal velvet can feel warm and enclosed.

Bouclé absorbs light, so its colour stays more even through the day. It looks gentle and calm, especially in creams, stones and soft greys. Because the texture does the work, bouclé often looks strongest on a simple headboard without heavy stitching or buttons.

If you are matching a bed to existing furniture, think about texture as well as colour. A velvet bed next to a velvet chair can look coordinated. A bouclé bed can add contrast to a room full of smooth surfaces.

Comfort and feel

Both fabrics feel soft against your skin when you sit up in bed to read. Velvet feels smooth and slightly cool at first touch. Bouclé feels thicker and more tactile. Neither changes how the mattress feels, because the fabric covers the headboard and base, not the sleeping surface. Choose the one you enjoy touching. You will lean against the headboard every day.

Pets and children

If you have a cat or dog that jumps on the bed, a short-pile velvet is usually the easier choice, because there are no loops for claws to catch. Pet hair shows on both fabrics, so pick a colour close to your pet's coat if you can. Bouclé's loops can snag on claws, jewellery or toys with sharp edges. If a loop pulls out, do not tug it. Push it gently back with a blunt needle, or snip it level with sharp scissors. For children's rooms, many parents like velvet for the same reason. Everyday care

The general rules are the same for both fabrics:

  • Vacuum regularly with a soft upholstery brush attachment to lift dust.
  • Blot spills straight away with a clean, dry cloth. Do not rub, because rubbing spreads the stain and can damage the pile or loops.
  • Check the care label, or ask us, before you use any cleaning product, and test it on a hidden area first.
  • Keep the bed out of strong direct sunlight where you can, because sunlight fades most fabrics over time.

For velvet, brush the pile gently in one direction to lift pressure marks. For bouclé, avoid stiff brushes that could pull the loops. Our article on caring for an upholstered bed has more tips.

Frequently asked questions

Is velvet or bouclé softer?

Both are soft. Velvet feels smooth and sleek, and bouclé feels thicker and more textured. The difference is in the type of softness, so try a swatch of each.

Which is better with pets, velvet or bouclé?

A short-pile velvet is usually easier with pets, because claws can catch in bouclé's loops. Pet hair shows on both, so a colour close to your pet's coat helps.

Does velvet show marks?

Velvet can show pressure marks and changes in pile direction. Brushing the pile gently in one direction usually lifts them. Bouclé's texture hides small marks more easily.

How do I clean a velvet or bouclé bed?

Vacuum with a soft upholstery brush, blot spills without rubbing, and follow the care label before using any cleaning product. Test anything new on a hidden area first.
