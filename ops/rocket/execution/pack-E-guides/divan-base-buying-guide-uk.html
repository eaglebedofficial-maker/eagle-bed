Divan Base Buying Guide: Drawers, Ottoman or Plain?

The short answer: a divan base is a box-shaped, fully upholstered base that sits directly under your mattress, with no separate slats or side rails. Choose a plain platform divan if you just want a neat, solid base. Choose a divan with drawers if you want storage you can reach without moving the mattress. Choose a gas lift ottoman divan if you want the most room for bulky items. Then match the size to your mattress and decide whether you want a headboard.

This guide explains each choice in plain terms, so you can pick the right divan for your room before you look at fabrics and colours.

What is a divan bed?

A divan bed has two parts: the divan base and the mattress on top. Most people add a headboard as a third part. The base is a closed, upholstered box, so you see fabric all the way to the floor instead of legs and an open space underneath. That is the main visual difference between a divan and a bed frame. A bed frame has rails, slats and usually a gap under the bed.

Because the base is enclosed, a divan is a practical way to use the space under the mattress. You can keep that space closed off from dust, or you can turn it into storage. If you are still deciding between the two styles, our article Divan Bed vs Bed Frame compares them side by side.

The three types of divan base

1. Plain platform divan

A platform divan has a firm, flat top and no built-in storage. It suits guest rooms, rooms where the space under the bed does not matter, or anyone who wants the simplest option. The Small Line Divan Bed is an example: a complete divan set with a small line panel headboard on a firm platform base.

2. Divan base with drawers

Drawers pull straight out from the side or foot of the base, so you can get to bedding, clothes or spare linen without lifting anything. This works well for things you use every week. The one thing to check is floor space: drawers need clear floor in front of them to open fully, so measure from the side of the bed to the nearest wall or wardrobe. Our Divan Storage Base with Drawers and the wider storage beds with drawers range are built this way. The number and position of drawers can vary by size and design, so check the product page or ask us before you order.

3. Gas lift ottoman divan

An ottoman divan has a top that lifts up on gas struts, which reveals the whole area under the mattress. You get one large storage space instead of separate drawers, which suits bulky or occasional items such as spare duvets, suitcases and seasonal clothes. You do not need floor space beside the bed, but you do need headroom above it for the mattress and platform to lift. The Ottoman Gas Lift Divan Base has a base height of 14 inches. Choosing the right size

Divan bases follow standard UK mattress sizes, so the simplest rule is to buy the base in the same size as your mattress. These are the usual UK mattress sizes:

  • Single (3ft): 90 x 190 cm
  • Small double (4ft): 120 x 190 cm
  • Double (4ft6): 135 x 190 cm
  • King (5ft): 150 x 200 cm
  • Super king (6ft): 180 x 200 cm

Eagle Bed divan bases are made in Single to Super King. Before you order, measure the room as well as the mattress. Allow space to walk around the bed, plus space for open drawers if you choose a drawer base. It also helps to check the route into the room (stairs, landings and doorways). Which mattress works on a divan?

A divan's firm top takes any standard UK mattress in the matching size, whether pocket sprung, memory foam or pillow top. Choose by feel. A pocket sprung mattress usually feels more springy and breathable. Memory foam moulds to your shape. A pillow top adds a softer layer on the surface. If you have an ottoman divan, remember that you lift the mattress each time you open it, so a lighter mattress is easier to live with. Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between a divan base and a bed frame?

A divan base is a closed, upholstered box that sits under the mattress. A bed frame uses rails and slats and usually stands on legs with a gap underneath. A divan gives a solid, streamlined look and makes it easy to add storage.

Can I put any mattress on a divan base?

Yes, as long as it is the same UK size as the base. Pocket sprung, memory foam and pillow top mattresses all work on a firm divan top.

Should I choose drawers or an ottoman divan?

Choose drawers for things you use often and if you have clear floor beside the bed. Choose a gas lift ottoman for bulky items and if floor space beside the bed is tight.
