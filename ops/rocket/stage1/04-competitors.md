## 5. Eagle Bed's genuine openings

1. **Price position (VERIFIED own; HISTORICAL rivals):** the King gas-lift base at £305.99 and panel frames at £349.99 sit in the middle of the handmade group (PSCL £249–£589, Stylo from £359.98, UK Beds Direct £399.99–£449.99, Divan Base Direct £509.95+) and far below John Lewis (£1,149+). Mass retailers (Furniture123, Dunelm) undercut on flat-pack ottomans, but those are not made to order.
2. **Speed:** "usually 3–5 working days" is faster than the stated standard of every handmade rival found (PSCL 5–10, UK Beds Direct 7–14, Divan Base Direct 3–10). PSCL charges extra for 3–5 days. This is Eagle Bed's clearest edge.
3. **Free GB delivery by our own team, including Scotland.** Bedwick lists England, Wales and Ireland only; Somni ships flat-pack for self-assembly; Dunelm charges below a threshold.
4. **Customer's own fabric accepted (owner-confirmed).** No competitor found states this. Say "we will confirm the fabric quantity for your size".
5. **Handmade in Dewsbury, family-run, visible workshop town.** PSCL's own pages are unclear whether frames are made in-house. A real workshop is a trust story.
6. **Payment choice:** Klarna, Clearpay, PayPal, card, cash on delivery. Cash on delivery was not seen at any rival.
7. **Swatches available**; UK Beds Direct charges £3.99 per sample. (Eagle Bed's swatch price: not checked, treat as UNKNOWN until confirmed.)
8. **5-year frame warranty**, versus PSCL 24 months and UK Beds Direct 2 years (Stylo also 5 years).

## 6. Eagle Bed's weaknesses

1. **Reviews (biggest gap).** PSCL has about 2,276 Trustpilot reviews at 5.0, and a Batley showroom seller has 692 at 4.9. Eagle Bed shows 1 Judge.me product review in the first 100 products checked and no Trustpilot profile found. No stars in Google results.
2. **Search visibility:** not in the top 20 for "ottoman bed uk" or "upholstered bed uk". Somni, Bedwick and Stylo (all similar size) rank on page 1.
3. **Content depth:** rivals use buyer tools and guides (Bedwick room-fit calculator; Stylo guides on gas lifts, frame types, bed sizes). Eagle has 26 blog posts; quality versus theirs not assessed here.
4. **Brand confusion:** a rival sells a product called "Eagle Bed Frame" that ranks for our name.
5. **Assembly is paid (£69.99)** while Divan Base Direct includes room-of-choice delivery free. Not unusual (PSCL also charges), but worth knowing when comparing "free delivery" claims.
6. **Returns:** 14-day free returns is good; no sleep trial. Rivals found also have no trial, so this is neutral.
