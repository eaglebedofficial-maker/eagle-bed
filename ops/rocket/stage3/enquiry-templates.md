# Eagle Bed enquiry and review templates (Stage 3, 19 Nov 2026)

These templates are safe to send as written. They use only facts from `ops/BRIEF.md`. There are no fill-in brackets. Where we do not know an answer yet, the template uses a holding line and a promise of when we will reply. The person sending may add the customer's first name after "Hello" and may add a sentence with a real price or date **only when the owner has confirmed it**.

Rules for every reply:
- Reply within one working day, Monday to Friday.
- Never promise free assembly. Never quote assembly at any price other than £69.99. Never mention Clearpay (not confirmed), 0%/interest-free, a sleep trial, "mainland", or any spec, material or certification that is not in the BRIEF.
- Never promise a delivery date the workshop has not confirmed.
- Sign off as "Eagle Bed". Phone/WhatsApp 07417 439197, Mon–Fri 9am–6pm.
- For WhatsApp, use the same text and drop the subject line.
