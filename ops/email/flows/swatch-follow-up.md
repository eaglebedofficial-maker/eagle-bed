# Swatch follow-up emails (sent by hand)

Send these yourself from eaglebedofficial@gmail.com. They are plain text, so copy from "Subject" to the sign-off and paste straight into Gmail.

Before you press send, replace the parts in **{curly brackets}**:
- **{first name}**: the customer's first name
- **{fabric list}**: the swatches you posted, e.g. "Grey, Silver and Navy"
- **{bed name}** and **{bed link}**: only if you know which bed they looked at. If not, delete that line.

Search the email for `{` before sending. If any curly bracket is left, it isn't ready.

Timing:
- Email 1: 3–4 days after you post the swatches
- Email 2: 7 days after email 1, only if they haven't replied or ordered
- After email 2, stop. Don't send a third.

---

## Notes

- These are one-to-one replies to a customer who asked for swatches, so they are service emails. Don't add these customers to a newsletter list unless they ticked to receive marketing.
- If the customer replies "not for now", thank them and stop.
- Keep a simple note (Gmail label "Swatch follow-up" works) of who got email 1 and on what date, so email 2 goes out on time.
