## The 15 spoken Q&As

### 1. What's the difference between an ottoman bed and a divan bed?

An ottoman bed lifts its whole base on gas pistons, so the space under the mattress becomes one big storage area. A divan is a fabric-covered base that can have drawers, an ottoman lift or no storage at all. Ottomans suit bulky items; drawers suit everyday things.

### 2. What is a divan bed?

A divan bed is a fabric-covered base, usually a wooden platform, that sits close to the floor and takes your mattress. It is normally paired with a headboard and often has built-in drawers or an ottoman gas lift for storage. Divans give a neat, compact look that suits smaller rooms.

### 3. Is a divan better than a bed frame?

It depends on the room. A divan is better for storage, with drawers or an ottoman lift, and feels firm and solid. An upholstered bed frame stands on legs, has a slatted base for airflow and makes a bigger statement. Pick a divan for storage, a frame for style.

### 4. How does an ottoman or gas lift bed work?

An ottoman bed has gas pistons on each side of the base, like an office chair. You lift the base by its handle, the pistons take the weight of the mattress and hold it open, and you reach all the storage underneath. To close it, guide the base back down.

## Ready-to-apply blocks

How to apply (owner or theme editor):

- Paste the HTML block near the top of the page body, just below the first paragraph, so the short answer is easy to find.
- Paste the JSON-LD script into the same page (page or article body in HTML mode, or the template). Each JSON-LD holds only the questions in its own block.
- On the 4 blog posts that already have a 'Frequently asked questions' list, and on the delivery page's 'Delivery questions' list, remove the old question that says the same thing, so the page does not repeat itself.
- For blog posts, the JSON-LD can sit next to the existing Article schema. There is no FAQPage schema on any of these pages yet, so there is no clash.
- Google now shows FAQ rich results mainly for government and health sites, so do not expect FAQ dropdowns in Google. The schema still helps assistants and AI answers read the page.
- Back up each page first and log every change in ops/CHANGELOG.md.
