Love a fabric but want to see it in person first? We completely understand. Choosing the right colour and texture for your bed is a big decision, so we’re happy to send you free fabric swatches to view in your own home, in your own light.

To request your free swatches, email us at eaglebedofficial@gmail.com or call us on +44 7417 439197 with the fabrics or colours you’d like to see, along with your name and delivery address. There’s no cost and no obligation to buy.

Once your swatches arrive, take your time. When you’re ready, you can choose your fabric directly on any product page.

Which fabrics can I ask for?

  • Plush velvet — soft pile with a deep, rich colour.
  • Chenille — hard-wearing with a textured, matt finish.
  • Coniston, Naples and Galaxy — our woven fabric ranges.
  • Teddy bouclé — cosy, modern and heavily textured.

Custom fabric and colour options are available on request — just ask when you email us.

What happens after you ask

  1. We reply to confirm the fabrics you’ve chosen and the address we’re posting to.
  2. Your swatches go out free of charge — there is nothing to pay and nothing to return.
  3. We’ll follow up once to check they arrived and answer any questions about fabric, size or mattress fit. If you’d rather we didn’t, just say so in your reply and we won’t.

Your details and your privacy

We use your name and address only to post your swatches and to answer your question. Asking for swatches does not sign you up to marketing emails. If you would also like occasional Eagle Bed news and new arrivals, tell us so in your email and we’ll add you — and you can ask us to stop at any time by replying “unsubscribe”. We never sell or share your details.
