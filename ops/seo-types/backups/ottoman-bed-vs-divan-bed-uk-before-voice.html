How Ottoman Beds Work

Ottoman beds use a gas-powered hydraulic lift. You lift the mattress platform and it stays open while you reach the large hollow storage space underneath. The whole base is one open compartment, which gives flexible storage for bulky items such as duvets, suitcases and seasonal clothing.

Ottoman beds open from the end or from the side, so you can choose the option that suits your room layout. Browse the Ottoman Beds collection to compare designs, or read how ottoman beds work. If you already have a headboard, the Ottoman Gas Lift Divan Base adds ottoman storage underneath it.

How Divan Drawer Beds Work

Divan drawer bases have built-in drawers on the side or end of the base. You pull each drawer out to reach the storage, which suits organised storage of smaller items you use more often.

Key Differences

Storage: an ottoman gives one large open space across the whole base, while drawers give several smaller compartments. Ottomans suit bulky items; drawers suit everyday ones.

Access: ottoman beds need clear floor space at the foot or side of the bed to operate the lift. Drawer divans need room to pull the drawers out.

Look: ottoman beds have no visible drawer handles, which gives a clean, seamless finish.

Support and comfort: both give a sturdy, even base for the mattress and work with pocket sprung, memory foam or orthopaedic mattresses. See our Mattresses collection.

Which Should You Choose?

  • Choose an ottoman bed if you want the most storage and a seamless look.
  • Choose a divan with drawers if you prefer organised compartments or your room layout makes lifting a platform awkward.

Frequently asked questions

Are ottoman beds better than divan beds?

Neither is better for everyone. Ottoman beds give more open storage for bulky items and a seamless look; divans with drawers give organised compartments. The right one depends on what you store and the floor space you have.

What is the difference between an ottoman and a divan bed?

An ottoman bed lifts the whole base on gas pistons to reveal one large storage space. A divan bed is a fabric-covered base that can have drawers, an ottoman lift or no storage at all.

Which has more storage, an ottoman or a divan with drawers?

An ottoman gives one large open space across the whole base, which suits bulky items. Drawers split the space into smaller compartments that are easier for everyday items.

Can I use the same mattress on either?

Yes. Both work with pocket sprung, memory foam or orthopaedic mattresses of the correct size.

What sizes do your ottoman and drawer bases come in?

Single (3FT), Small Double (4FT), Double (4FT6), King (5FT) and Super King (6FT). See the UK bed sizes guide for dimensions.
