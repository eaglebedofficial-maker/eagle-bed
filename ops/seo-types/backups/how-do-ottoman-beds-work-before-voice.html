The gas-lift mechanism

An ottoman bed uses a gas-lift (hydraulic) mechanism, the same kind of technology found in an office chair. Gas pistons sit on either side of the base. When you lift the base using the handle at the foot or side of the bed, the pistons take the weight of the mattress and hold it in the raised position, so lifting takes little effort. To close it, guide the base back down and the weight of the mattress helps it settle into place.

How much storage do you get?

Because the entire base lifts, you get storage across the whole footprint of the bed, which is more open space than a bed with drawers. It suits bulky items like spare duvets, pillows, suitcases and seasonal clothing.

Side-opening vs end-opening

End-opening beds lift from the foot of the bed. Side-opening beds lift from one side, which suits a room where the foot of the bed sits against a wall or the room is narrow. Choose whichever suits your layout. The Side Lift Ottoman Gas Lift Storage Bed is an example of a side-opening design.

Is it sturdy?

The gas pistons hold the base in the raised position and the solid wood frame keeps everything stable. Guide the base up and down rather than forcing it. Check the mattress depth against the product page, as a very thick mattress can affect how the base closes and how far it opens against the headboard.

Before you buy: a quick checklist

  • Is there clear floor space at the foot or side of the bed for the base to lift? Choose end- or side-opening to suit.
  • Does your mattress depth match what the product page recommends?
  • Will the open base clear the headboard and any bedside furniture?
  • Do you want a full bed with a headboard, or just a base to go under a headboard you already own?

Frequently asked questions

What does a gas lift bed mean?

A gas lift bed has gas pistons that raise the mattress platform and hold it open, giving access to storage underneath without you having to take the weight yourself.

What is a gas lift storage bed?

A gas lift storage bed is an ottoman bed. Gas pistons lift the whole mattress platform and hold it open, so the space under the bed becomes one large storage compartment for duvets, suitcases and seasonal clothing.

How does an ottoman bed work?

You lift the base by its handle, the gas pistons take the weight of the mattress and hold the base open, and you reach the storage underneath. Guide the base back down to close it.

Does the base stay open on its own?

Yes. The gas pistons hold the base in the raised position until you guide it back down.

Is it hard to lift an ottoman bed?

No. The pistons take the weight of the mattress, so lifting takes little effort.
