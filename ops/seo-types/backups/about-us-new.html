Our Dewsbury workshop

Every Eagle Bed is built and upholstered to order in our own workshop in Dewsbury. Each bed is made for one customer, in the size, fabric and colour that customer chooses, so the bed that arrives is made for your room rather than taken off a shelf.

How a bed is made

  1. You choose. Pick a design, a size (Kids Single 2ft6, Single 3ft, Small Double 4ft, Double 4ft6, King 5ft or Super King 6ft), a fabric and a colour.
  2. We build the frame. Each bed is built on a solid wood frame with a slatted base. Ottoman beds can have a gas-lift base that lifts the whole mattress platform for storage underneath.
  3. We upholster it by hand. The frame and headboard are covered by hand in your chosen fabric, with details such as button tufting on our Chesterfield designs or stitched channels on our panel designs.
  4. We deliver it. Our own team brings it to your door, usually within 3 to 5 working days of your order.
