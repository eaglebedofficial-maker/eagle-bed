Handmade beds delivered across Leeds — from a workshop just up the road

Every Eagle Bed is built by hand in our workshop on Pepproyd Street in Dewsbury, roughly 15 miles from Leeds city centre. So when you order an upholstered bed for a home in Headingley, Chapel Allerton, Roundhay or anywhere across the LS postcodes, it hasn't travelled far to reach you — it's made to order a short drive away.

What we make

We specialise in handmade upholstered beds in plush velvet, chenille and bouclé: wingback beds with tall winged headboards, ottoman gas-lift storage beds, classic divan bases and deep-buttoned Chesterfield frames. Every frame runs from Single to Super King, with matching headboards to finish the room.

Delivery to Leeds

Delivery across Leeds is free, typically within our standard 3–5 working days. Because you're close to the workshop, we can often be flexible on your delivery day — just tell us what suits. Your bed arrives fully made, with no flat-pack assembly.
