The Prism Geometric Bed has an angular geometric headboard shape and sits at the top of our range. Its lines are sharper than the Aurora Fan Panel design, built for bedrooms that want something more architectural.

Key features

  • Angular geometric headboard and matching footboard
  • Handcrafted in the UK
  • Solid timber frame with a slatted base for support and ventilation
  • Optional ottoman gas-lift storage
  • Choice of Plush Velvet, Naples, Chenille, Linen or Crushed Velvet

Sizes

Available in Single (3FT), Small Double (4FT), Double (4FT6), King (5FT) and Super King (6FT).
