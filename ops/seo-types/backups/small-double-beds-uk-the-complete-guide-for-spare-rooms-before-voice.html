Small Double Beds UK — The Complete Guide for Spare Rooms

A small double (4FT, 120 x 190 cm) is 15 cm narrower than a standard double (4FT6, 135 x 190 cm) and 30 cm wider than a single. It suits spare rooms, guest rooms and teenagers' bedrooms where floor space matters more than a full-width bed. It gives a single sleeper plenty of room and can take two people for occasional use.

By the Eagle Bed workshop team, Dewsbury. Last updated: 20 October 2026.

Small Double vs Standard Double

Small double (4FT) Double (4FT6)
Mattress size 120 x 190 cm 135 x 190 cm
Width per person if shared About 60 cm each About 67 cm each
Minimum wall width with 60 cm each side 240 cm 255 cm
Best for Spare rooms, one sleeper, occasional guests Two adults in an average bedroom

A small double is 4FT (120cm) wide. A standard double is 4FT6 (135cm) wide, which is 15cm wider. In a smaller bedroom, 15cm can make a real difference to the floor space around the bed. See all sizes in our UK bed sizes guide, or read how to measure your bedroom for a new bed.

Frequently asked questions

What size is a small double bed?

4FT wide, with a mattress of 120 x 190 cm.

What is the difference between a small double and a double?

A double (4FT6) is 135 cm wide, 15 cm wider than a small double. Both are 190 cm long.

Can two people sleep in a small double?

Yes, for occasional use, with about 60 cm each. For regular use by two adults, a double or king gives more room.

Do you make a small double ottoman bed?

Will a small double fit my spare room?

Allow at least 60 cm of floor each side you walk down, which means a wall width of about 240 cm. Call 07417 439197 (Monday to Friday, 9am to 6pm) if you would like help checking.
