Should I choose a gas lift ottoman bed or a divan with drawers?

A gas lift ottoman lifts the whole mattress platform on hydraulic struts, so you reach the full under-bed area without moving the mattress. A divan with drawers has fixed drawers: less total space, but no lifting. Our ottoman bed buying guide explains the trade-offs, including when an ottoman is the wrong choice.

How much space do I need to open an ottoman bed?

End-lift bases open from the foot of the bed, so keep the floor clear there. Side-lift bases open from one side, which suits a bed with its foot against a wall. Either way you need room above the bed to swing the platform open.

How do I know a bed will fit my room?

Measure the wall the bed will sit against, then the floor space in front, and leave at least 60cm of walking space at one side. Our UK bed sizes guide lists every mattress size, or email eaglebedofficial@gmail.com with your room measurements.
