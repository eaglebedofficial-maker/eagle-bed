The best bed for a small bedroom is one that keeps the floor clear: an ottoman bed or a divan with drawers gives storage without extra furniture, and a small double (4FT) or single (3FT) leaves more walking space than a larger bed. Measure your wall width first, then use the table below to see which sizes leave room to walk around the bed.

For a more detailed guide to what each storage bed needs in terms of floor space, see How to Choose a Storage Bed for a Small UK Bedroom. This page gives a quick overview.

Which bed size fits your room width?

Allow at least 60 cm of clear floor on each side you will walk down. Adding the mattress width to 60 cm either side gives the minimum wall width (the same method as our UK bed sizes guide):

Size Mattress width Minimum wall width with 60 cm each side
Single (3FT) 90 cm 210 cm
Small Double (4FT) 120 cm 240 cm
Double (4FT6) 135 cm 255 cm
King (5FT) 150 cm 270 cm
Super King (6FT) 180 cm 300 cm

Allow extra for the headboard and the upholstered sides of the frame, and 60 cm or more at the foot. If the bed sits against a wall on one side you need less width, but you must still reach both sides to make it. Our guide to measuring your bedroom shows the method step by step. Once you know your size, you can shop double beds, king size beds or super king beds.

1. Ottoman storage beds

An ottoman bed is a strong space-saver. The base lifts on gas pistons to reveal storage across the whole footprint of the bed, so you can store duvets, suitcases and out-of-season clothes without extra furniture. Choose a side-opening ottoman if the foot of your bed sits against a wall.

2. Divan beds with drawers

A divan bed with built-in drawers keeps everyday items close to hand and takes up no more room than the bed itself. See also our storage beds.

Ottoman vs drawers in a small room

Ottoman bed Divan with drawers
Storage One large open space under the whole base Several smaller compartments
Floor space to leave clear At the foot (end-lift) or side (side-lift) for the lift At the side or end to pull the drawers out
Best for Duvets, suitcases, seasonal clothes Everyday items you reach for often

If the foot of the bed is against a wall, choose a side-lift ottoman or drawers on the side. Weighing the two? Read our ottoman vs divan comparison.

Tips to make a small bedroom feel bigger

  • Choose a bed with hidden storage to keep the floor clear.
  • Pick a lighter fabric colour to reflect more light.
  • Keep the area under and around the bed tidy.
  • A tall, upholstered headboard draws the eye upward.

Frequently asked questions

What size bed fits a small bedroom?

It depends on the wall width. With 60 cm of walking space each side, a single needs 210 cm, a small double 240 cm and a double 255 cm.

Is an ottoman bed good for a small room?

Yes, if you can leave clear floor space at the foot or side for the base to lift. A side-lift design suits a bed with its foot against a wall.

Ottoman bed or divan with drawers for a small room?

Ottoman gives one large space for bulky items; drawers suit everyday items. Pick by what you store and where you can leave space to open it.

Is a small double big enough for one adult?

A small double is 120 x 190 cm, 30 cm wider than a single, and is a common upgrade for a spare room or small second bedroom.
