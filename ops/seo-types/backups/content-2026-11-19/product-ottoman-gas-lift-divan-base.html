What is a gas lift bed?

A gas lift bed uses hydraulic gas struts to lift the whole mattress platform on a hinge, opening up a large storage compartment underneath without needing to move the mattress off first. It's the most space-efficient way to add hidden storage to a UK bedroom, from £220.99.

Ottoman bed vs divan bed

A divan bed is an upholstered box base, usually with drawers or with no storage at all. An ottoman bed is a divan whose whole mattress platform lifts on gas struts, opening the full footprint of the bed as storage. Eagle Bed's gas lift base starts at £220.99 in Single 3ft.
