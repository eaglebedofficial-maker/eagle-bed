At Eagle Bed, we believe a great night’s sleep shouldn’t have to wait. That’s why we offer flexible ways to pay, so you can spread the cost of your new bed in a way that suits you.
Klarna – Pay Later
Klarna lets you buy now and pay later, spreading the cost into manageable instalments. Simply choose Klarna at checkout and follow the steps. Approval is subject to Klarna’s terms and eligibility checks.
Clearpay – Pay in Instalments
Clearpay allows you to split your purchase into instalments, paid over time. Select Clearpay at checkout to see your payment schedule. Subject to Clearpay’s terms and eligibility.
Cash on Delivery
Prefer to pay when your bed arrives? Cash on delivery is available. Please call us on 07417 439197 or email us before you order, and we will arrange it for you.
Coming soon: DivideBuy
We are working on adding DivideBuy as another way to spread the cost. It is not available yet; we will update this page as soon as it is.

Need help choosing?
If you have any questions about paying for your order, our team is happy to help. Call us on 07417 439197 or email eaglebedofficial@gmail.com, Monday to Friday, 9am to 6pm.
Please note: availability of each payment option may depend on your eligibility. Klarna and Clearpay are provided subject to the relevant provider’s terms and conditions.
