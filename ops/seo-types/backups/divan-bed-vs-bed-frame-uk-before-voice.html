What Is a Divan Bed?

A divan bed has a base unit, usually a wooden platform covered in fabric, paired with a headboard. The base sits close to the floor and often includes built-in drawers or an ottoman gas lift. Divans give a neat, compact look. See the range in our Divan Beds collection.

What Is a Bed Frame?

A bed frame is a freestanding structure with legs that holds the mattress. Upholstered bed frames have a padded fabric exterior and a statement headboard, and tend to have more visual impact when the bed is the centrepiece of the room. Browse our Upholstered Beds.

Storage

Divans are the stronger choice for storage. Built-in drawers and ottoman gas lift bases give convenient underbed storage that a standard bed frame does not. If your bedroom lacks wardrobe space, a divan with storage is the practical option. We offer drawer divans and ottoman divans in the Divan Ottoman Beds collection.

Style

Bed frames often make the stronger visual statement. An upholstered frame with a tall headboard, like the Mayfair Wingback or Vegas Luxury, becomes the focal point of the room.

Comfort

Comfort comes mainly from the mattress rather than the base. A platform-top divan feels firm and solid, while a slatted bed frame allows a little more mattress flex and airflow. See our Mattresses collection.

Price

Prices vary by size, design and fabric, so compare specific models rather than bed types. Delivery is free across Great Britain (Northern Ireland £90), and Klarna and Clearpay are available.

Which Should You Choose?

Choose a divan if storage is your priority or your room is small. Choose a bed frame if you want maximum visual impact and have storage elsewhere. If you want both, an ottoman divan combines storage with an upholstered design.

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between a divan bed and a bed frame?

A divan is a fabric-covered base that sits close to the floor, often with drawers or an ottoman lift. A bed frame is a freestanding structure on legs, and upholstered frames add a padded headboard and sides.

Platform bed frame vs divan base: which is better?

Neither is better for everyone. A divan base suits you if you want built-in storage and a compact, close-to-the-floor look. A platform bed frame with a slatted base suits you if you want the bed to be the centrepiece and have storage elsewhere. Comfort depends mainly on the mattress.

What is a divan bed base?

A divan bed base is the fabric-covered platform that holds your mattress. It can have drawers, an ottoman gas lift or no storage, and it is usually paired with a separate headboard.

Is a divan or a bed frame better for storage?

A divan, because built-in drawers or an ottoman gas lift give underbed storage that a standard bed frame does not.

Is a divan base or a platform bed frame more comfortable?

Comfort comes mainly from the mattress. A platform-top divan feels firm and solid, while a slatted frame gives slightly more flex and airflow.
