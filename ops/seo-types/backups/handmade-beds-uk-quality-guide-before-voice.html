Many beds on the market are flat-packed or mass-produced. A handmade UK bed is made differently: built to order, with the materials and finish chosen for you. Here is what to look for.

What Does Handmade in the UK Mean?

A handmade bed is built by people rather than a production line, usually to order. At Eagle Bed, every bed is made in our own workshop in Dewsbury, West Yorkshire, on a solid wood frame that is assembled and upholstered by hand.

Why Does the Frame Material Matter?

The frame is the foundation of any bed. Solid wood is a strong, traditional choice for bed frames. Some lower-priced beds use MDF or chipboard instead, so it is worth checking the frame material in any product description before you buy.

Why Does Upholstery Matter?

Upholstery decides how a bed looks and feels. We upholster in fabrics including plush velvet, chenille and teddy bouclé over padding for a soft finish. Free fabric swatches are available so you can judge the colour and texture first.

Can You Customise a Handmade Bed?

Because each bed is made to order, you can choose size, fabric and colour. If you want something specific, such as a different headboard height, contact us and we will tell you what we can do.

Warranty and Delivery

Our beds carry a 5-year warranty. Delivery is free across Great Britain by our own team (Northern Ireland £90), usually within 3 to 5 working days.
