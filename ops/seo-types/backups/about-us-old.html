A UK family business, handcrafting beds to order

Eagle Bed is a family-run British bed maker based in Dewsbury, West Yorkshire — the heart of the country's textile region. Every bed we sell is handmade to order in the UK. We don't import finished beds from overseas: each frame is built and upholstered here, for one customer at a time.

What we make

We specialise in upholstered beds — wingback beds, Chesterfield beds, ottoman storage beds, divan beds and children's beds — along with a range of UK-made mattresses. Each bed is built on a solid wood frame and wrapped in your choice of premium fabric, including plush velvet, chenille and soft woven bouclé finishes. You can choose your size (Kids Single 2ft6 through to Super King 6ft), colour and headboard style, so the bed that arrives is made for your room rather than pulled off a shelf.

Made properly, made to last

Because our beds are made to order, we can offer a level of choice in fabric, colour and size that mass-produced beds can't match — and we stand behind the build. Every Eagle Bed frame and headboard carries a 5-year limited manufacturer's warranty covering defects in materials and workmanship. Full terms are on our warranty page.
