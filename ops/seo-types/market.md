# Marketplaces for Eagle Bed: fees, fit and 5 draft listings (10 Nov 2026)

Job `market` from the SEO types plan. Research and drafts only: no accounts opened, no Shopify writes, no price changes.

## The short answer

**Start with Etsy UK, and apply to Amazon Handmade at the same time.** Leave eBay for a later test of the two ottoman bases. Skip OnBuy and ManoMano for now.

- Etsy has the lowest cost on a £400 bed (about £44 before VAT, no monthly fee), is built for handmade sellers, and lets us set our own delivery and making time.
- Amazon Handmade has the biggest UK buyer audience, the monthly fee is waived once approved, and 12.24% is a flat fee (about £49 on £400). It is the only Amazon route with a handmade badge and no barcodes needed.
- eBay costs about the same as Amazon Handmade but shows us next to cheap imported ottoman beds with no handmade badge.
- OnBuy (15% + £25/month) and ManoMano (15–25% + £50/month) cost more and their buyers are not looking for handmade beds.

### Fee sources (all accessed 10 Nov 2026)

**Etsy UK**
- Listing fee US$0.20 (shown as £0.15), renews each sale / every 4 months — https://www.etsy.com/uk/sell
- Transaction fee 6.5% of price incl. delivery — https://www.etsy.com/uk/legal/fees/
- Etsy Payments processing UK 4% + £0.20 per order — https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/115015628847-What-are-Payment-Processing-Fees-for-Selling-on-Etsy
- Regulatory operating fee UK 0.48% (up from 0.32% on 22 Jun 2026) — https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/1500011073202-What-is-a-Regulatory-Operating-Fee
- Offsite Ads 15% of attributed orders (opt-out under US$10k sales/yr), 12% compulsory at US$10k+, max US$100 per order — https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/360000338367-How-Etsy-s-Offsite-Ads-Work
- Payout: New sellers: funds usually available 14 days after a sale, paid weekly on Mondays by default; a reserve can apply to new shops (https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/360002080688-Why-Hasn-t-My-Money-Been-Deposited-Yet, accessed 2026-11-10).
- Delivery: Good. You set your own delivery profile (free to England, Scotland, Wales; £90 to Northern Ireland and islands) and your own processing time, so own-team 2-person delivery works. Keep proof of delivery (signature or photo) for any dispute.
- Handmade: Very good. Etsy's Creativity Standards allow items made by the seller and shop members with hand and power tools; furniture made in your own workshop fits. Shop members must be described in the shop's About section.

**Amazon Handmade (UK)**
- Referral fee 12.24% per sale (no other selling fee) — https://sell.amazon.co.uk/programmes/handmade
- Professional plan £25/month (excl. VAT) waived once approved for Handmade — https://sell.amazon.co.uk/programmes/handmade
- Payout: Funds released 7 days after delivery date (DD+7) as standard; Amazon can set a longer reserve for new accounts (Amazon staff post: https://sellercentral.amazon.co.uk/seller-forums/discussions/t/a3106511f780c0ef74b7d8e2360163f7, accessed 2026-11-10).
- Delivery: Workable but strict. Seller-fulfilled: Amazon requires at least 90% on-time delivery (OTDR, from 16 Sep 2025); the new accurate-handling-time rule (29 Jun 2026) does not apply to custom and handmade items. Your van team gives no carrier tracking number, so ask Seller Support how to confirm own-fleet deliveries before you list. Heavy & Bulky programme (>30 kg or >1.5 m) is invite-only.
- Handmade: Good, subject to approval. Products must be handmade, hand-altered or hand-assembled; mass-produced items are not allowed; third-party guides quote a limit of 20 or fewer employees (owner must confirm headcount and check Amazon's own policy page). Application is reviewed manually.

**Amazon.co.uk (standard catalogue)**
- Furniture referral fee 15% up to £175, 10% above £175, min £0.25 — https://sell.amazon.co.uk/pricing
- Professional plan £25/month excl. VAT — https://sell.amazon.co.uk/pricing
- Payout: As Amazon Handmade (DD+7).
- Delivery: Same delivery rules as Handmade (90% OTDR).
- Handmade: No handmade badge. Needs GTIN barcodes or a GTIN exemption / Brand Registry; Shopify has no barcodes on these products.

**eBay UK (business seller)**
- Home, Furniture & DIY final value fee 11.9% up to £500 per item, 7.9% above (page updated 5 Nov 2026) — https://www.ebay.co.uk/help/selling/fees-credits-invoices/fees-business-sellers?id=4809

- Payout: Usually available about 24 hours after payment, daily payouts by default; new business sellers can have holds of up to about 31 days (https://www.ebay.co.uk/help/selling/getting-paid/getting-paid-items-youve-sold?id=4814, accessed 2026-11-10).
