## The 20 pins

Every link uses `?utm_source=pinterest&utm_medium=social&utm_campaign=pins`, so in GA4 these show as `pinterest / social`, campaign `pins`. Every image URL is copied exactly from products.json.

**About the images.** This container is not allowed to download photos from cdn.shopify.com (brief rule), so I could not look at them. For each pin I used the product's featured image, which is the photo you chose to lead the listing and the one Pinterest also shows for Rich Pins. I left out swatch charts, the fabric guide, the delivery banner and the size screenshots. Before uploading, look at the 20 previews in Pinterest. If one is a plain cut-out rather than a room photo, swap in one of the `altImages` URLs listed for that pin in pin.json. Two to check closely: **P07 Cloud Teddy** (1344x796, landscape, which gets cropped on Pinterest) and **P10 Line Kids** (1206x880). Square images work. Tall 2:3 images (1000x1500) do best on Pinterest, so they are worth making later.
