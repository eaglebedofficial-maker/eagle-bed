Frequently asked questions

What mattress does a kids bed need?

A standard UK mattress in the matching size. Kids Single (2.6FT) beds need a 2ft6 mattress — check the product page before ordering.

Are the kids beds upholstered all round?

Our kids beds have padded, upholstered headboards and frames. See each product page for the details of that design.

How long does delivery take, and can I return a bed?

Delivery is free across Great Britain (England, Scotland and Wales), made by our own team, usually within 3–5 working days. Delivery to Northern Ireland is £90. Returns are free within 14 days if the item is unused, unassembled and in its original packaging, and a faulty item is replaced free or fully refunded. See our returns policy.

What warranty do I get?

Every Eagle Bed comes with a 5-year warranty. See our warranty page for the full terms.

Can I see the fabric before I order?

Yes. We post free fabric swatches so you can check colour and texture in your own light. Request free swatches.
