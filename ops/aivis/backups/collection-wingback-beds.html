Wingback bed buying guide

Why choose a wingback?

The wings give you something soft to lean against when sitting up, and a tall winged headboard makes the bed the focal point of the room. It suits a plain wall behind the bed.

Measuring for a wingback

The wings add to the width of the headboard, so measure the wall and leave room for bedside tables. Headboard height varies by design; check the product page for exact measurements.

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between a wingback and a panel bed?

A panel bed has a flat headboard divided into padded sections. A wingback adds forward-facing side wings. See our panel beds.

Can I get a wingback bed with storage?

Yes. Some designs come with drawers and others are offered with a gas-lift ottoman base — see storage beds.
