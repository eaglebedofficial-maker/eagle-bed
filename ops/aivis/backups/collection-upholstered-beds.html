Upholstered bed buying guide

1. Pick a headboard style

  • Panel beds — padded vertical, horizontal or fan-shaped sections for a clean, tailored look. See panel beds.
  • Wingback beds — a tall headboard with side wings that frame the bed. See wingback beds.
  • Chesterfield beds — classic deep button tufting. See Chesterfield beds.
  • Line, bumper and statement designs — including the Mayfair, Ambassador, Sunrise and Grand Princess ranges.

2. Decide if you need storage

A gas-lift ottoman bed lifts the whole mattress platform to reveal storage under the full bed, while a storage bed with drawers gives you pull-out drawers in the base. See all storage beds to compare.

3. Choose your size

Beds are made in Single (3FT), Small Double (4FT), Double (4FT6), King (5FT) and Super King (6FT). Check our UK bed sizes guide before you order and measure the room, including door and stair access. To shop by size, see double beds, king size beds and super king beds.

4. Choose your fabric and colour

Every bed is upholstered to order in Plush Velvet, Chenille, Coniston, Naples, Galaxy and Teddy Bouclé, in solid colours. Velvet shows off panel and button detailing; bouclé and chenille give a softer, textured finish.

Any standard UK mattress in the matching size will fit. You can add one of our mattresses to complete the bed.

What warranty do I get?

Every Eagle Bed comes with a 5-year warranty. See our warranty page for the full terms.

Can I see the fabric before I order?

Yes. We post free fabric swatches so you can check colour and texture in your own light. Request free swatches.
