A panel bed has a headboard divided into padded sections, such as tall vertical panels, horizontal channels or a fan shape, for a tailored modern look. Ours are handmade to order in Dewsbury on solid wood frames, from Single to Super King, delivered free across Great Britain.

Not quite right? For tall side wings see wingback beds; for deep buttoning see Chesterfield beds.

Panel bed buying guide

Panel styles

  • Vertical panel — Eagle Mayfair Panel Bed, Eagle Chelsea Panel Bed, Eagle Monarch Panel Bed and Hampton Panel Bed.
  • Extra-tall panel — Grand Extra-Tall Panel Bed for rooms with high ceilings.
  • Fan and geometric — Eagle Sunrise Fan Panel Bed, Amber Sunrise Panel Bed and Prism Geometric Bed.
  • Gold trim — Eagle Regal Gold-Trim Bed and Regal Goldline Panel Bed.
  • Channel and fluted — Eagle Savoy Channel Bed and Fluted Velvet Bed.

Headboard height

Taller panel headboards suit high ceilings; shorter designs suit lower ceilings and smaller rooms. Check the measurements on each product page and the wall space you have.

Fabrics

Upholstered to order in Plush Velvet, Chenille, Coniston, Naples, Galaxy and Teddy Bouclé. Velvet shows panel lines most strongly; bouclé and chenille give a softer, textured look.
