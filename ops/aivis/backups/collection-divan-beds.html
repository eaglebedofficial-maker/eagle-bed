Divan bed buying guide

Which type of divan?

  • Divan with drawers — drawers built into the base for everyday items. See the Divan Storage Base with Drawers and our storage beds with drawers.
  • Ottoman divan — the platform lifts on gas struts for storage under the whole bed. See the Ottoman Gas Lift Divan Base (end lift) and the Side Lift Ottoman Storage Base.
  • Divan bed sets — base and headboard together, such as the Small Line Divan Bed, Brooklyn Divan Bed, Divan Bed with Footboard and Multi Mini Line Divan Ottoman Bed.

Measuring up

Measure the wall the bed will sit against and the floor space around it. Drawers need clear floor in front of them to open; an ottoman lid needs clear height above the bed.

Sizes and fabrics

Made in Single (3FT), Small Double (4FT), Double (4FT6), King (5FT) and Super King (6FT), upholstered in Plush Velvet, Chenille, Coniston, Naples, Galaxy and Teddy Bouclé. See our UK bed sizes guide.

Frequently asked questions

Do divan beds come with a headboard?

Divan bases can be ordered on their own, or as a set with a matching upholstered headboard — check each product page. You can also choose a separate headboard from our headboards collection.

Divan with drawers or ottoman?

Drawers are easier for things you use every day. An ottoman gives more space for bulky items such as duvets and suitcases, but needs room above the bed to open.
