How to choose a Chesterfield bed

Start with the look you want. A classic Chesterfield bed has buttoned tufting across the whole headboard, while some designs mix button tufting with panel detail or add tall wings. Then decide on fabric: Plush Velvet shows off the buttoning most, and Chenille, Coniston, Naples, Galaxy and Teddy Bouclé give a softer finish. Check the size you need against our UK bed sizes guide, and if you want storage, look for designs offered with a gas-lift ottoman base. Several Chesterfield designs have a matching footboard, so allow for the extra length when you measure the room. Free fabric swatches let you check colour and texture at home before you order.

Deep button tufting on the headboard, usually in a diamond pattern, taken from the traditional Chesterfield sofa.

What size of room does a Chesterfield bed need?

A Chesterfield bed has a tall tufted headboard and some designs have a matching footboard, so measure the wall and the floor length as well as the mattress size. Our guide to measuring your bedroom shows how.

Is a Chesterfield bed hard to keep clean?

Vacuum the buttoned areas regularly with a soft brush attachment to lift dust from the tufting.
