Storage bed buying guide

Ottoman (gas lift) storage

Also called a lift-up storage bed: the mattress platform lifts on gas struts to reveal one large compartment. Best for bulky, occasional items such as spare duvets, suitcases and seasonal clothes. Needs clear space above the bed to open. See ottoman beds, including end-lift and side-lift options, or shop by size: small double ottoman beds and king size ottoman beds.

Drawer storage

Drawers built into the base, ideal for things you use every day. Needs clear floor space in front of the drawers. See storage beds with drawers.

Frequently asked questions

Which gives more storage: ottoman or drawers?

An ottoman uses the space under the whole bed in one compartment. Drawers divide the space into sections that are quicker to reach every day.

Can I use my own mattress?

Yes. Any standard UK mattress in the matching size will fit.
