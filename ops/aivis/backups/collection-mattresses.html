Mattress buying guide

Mattress types

  • Pocket sprung — individually wrapped springs that respond separately to movement. Choose from the 1000, 2000 or 3000 Pocket Sprung Mattress.
  • Memory foam — foam that moulds to your shape. See the Memory Foam Mattress.
  • Orthopaedic — a firmer, more supportive feel. See the Memory Orthopaedic Mattress.
  • Pillow top — an extra quilted comfort layer on top. See the Pillow Top Mattress.

Firmness and sleeping position

As a general guide, side sleepers often prefer a softer surface that cushions the shoulders and hips, while back and front sleepers often prefer firmer support. If you share a bed, a pocket sprung mattress can reduce the feeling of your partner moving.
