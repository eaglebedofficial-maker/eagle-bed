Questions

What size is a small double bed?

A small double is 4ft wide and takes a standard UK small double mattress of 120 x 190cm. A standard double is 4ft6 (135 x 190cm).

Will a storage bed fit in a box room?

Measure first. A side lift ottoman can sit with its foot against a wall but needs about 60cm of walking space on one side. A drawer bed needs about 60cm of clear floor on the drawer side.

Do the beds take a standard mattress?

Yes. Each base takes a standard UK mattress in the size you order, and our mattresses come in Small Double 4ft too.
